2. Information you provide or create

Depending on how you use FitTrack, information can include:

  • Account details such as your name and email address, and sign-in provider information.
  • Fitness and wellness records you enter, including routines, workout history, activity, steps, body measurements, nutrition and food logs, water intake, goals, and schedules.
  • Meal photos you choose from your gallery or take with your camera to scan food and estimate ingredients and nutrition.
  • App preferences, such as units, theme, and reminder settings.

Weight and calorie records are account data and stay on your account. They are saved locally on your device and synchronized to Firebase only for accounts linked to cloud services; they are not shared with other users.

FitTrack does not need access to your entire photo library: you choose an individual image using the device’s gallery picker. Camera access is used only when you choose the camera option.

3. Local storage and cloud synchronization

FitTrack saves account and fitness information locally on your device so local features can work without a cloud connection. Email/password accounts have a password verifier stored locally; the verifier and plaintext password are excluded from FitTrack’s cloud snapshot.

Cloud synchronization is available for accounts linked to Firebase. When linked, FitTrack synchronizes account profile and fitness records to Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore so they can be restored and used across devices. A locally created email account can remain device-only unless it is linked to cloud services. Google sign-in uses Firebase Authentication and may provide your Google account email, display name, and profile photo URL.

Meal photos selected for scanning are compressed on your device and sent to the authenticated Cloudflare Worker for analysis. The Worker processes photos in memory and does not save or attach them to your food log. Food entries created by older app versions may still contain previously uploaded Firebase Storage photo links.

4. How information is used

FitTrack uses this information to provide sign-in, save and display your records, calculate the summaries and goals shown in the app, synchronize linked accounts, restore data across devices, and troubleshoot or protect the Service. We do not currently include advertising or analytics SDKs in the app.

5. Service providers

FitTrack uses Google Firebase services for authentication and cloud database synchronization, and Cloudflare Workers to host the food-analysis API. The Worker verifies Firebase Authentication ID tokens before processing requests. Google and Cloudflare process information as service providers under their applicable terms and privacy documentation. When you choose Google sign-in, Google and Firebase may process information needed to authenticate you. See Firebase privacy and security, Google’s Privacy Policy, and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

Before your first scan, FitTrack explains that your compressed meal photo and, when you edit the detected list, ingredient text are sent to Google Gemini for food recognition or photo comparison. Ingredient names are sent to USDA FoodData Central for nutrition lookup. If USDA has no matching nutrition data, the ingredient names may also be sent to Gemini for an identified estimate. The Worker does not store meal photos or meal records, and FitTrack does not cache these nutrition responses in Firestore. Google Gemini, USDA, and Cloudflare process requests under their respective service terms and privacy policies; review those current terms for information about provider-side processing and retention. See Google’s Gemini API terms, Google’s Privacy Policy, and USDA’s FoodData Central API guide.

When you open these public policy pages, Firebase Hosting necessarily receives the request and may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site.

6. Retention and deletion

Locally stored records remain on the device until they are removed, the app’s data is cleared, or the device removes them. Signing out preserves the account’s local records. The app prunes dated water records after 90 days, archived daily logs after 365 entries, and dated workout history older than 365 days; other account information remains until changed or removed.

Cloud copies remain in Firebase while associated with the account unless deleted by an authorized process. FitTrack currently has no in-app control to delete an account or all cloud information. Removing a food log item does not currently delete the corresponding image file from Firebase Storage. To request deletion or ask about your information, contact [privacy contact email]. Signing out or uninstalling the app alone does not delete Firebase data.

7. Security

FitTrack uses Firebase Authentication and owner-scoped Firestore and Storage rules to restrict linked cloud data to the signed-in account. No method of local or online storage is completely secure; keep your device and account credentials protected. Local-only records are not backed up by FitTrack unless you link the account.
